## The entry (185 characters, one sentence)

> On a rainy afternoon at my grandmother's house, her rendang fell apart in thick coconut milk, and the smell of lemongrass on the steam still pulls me straight back to her kitchen table.

## Why it works

This is one person's specific memory, not a stock line about a great meal. It names the dish (grandmother's rendang), the place (her house, her kitchen table), the texture (beef falling apart in thick coconut milk), the smell (lemongrass on the steam), and one exact moment (a rainy afternoon). The ending lands by pulling the narrator — and the reader — straight back to that table.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no clock) writes the single entry line to `entry.txt` in the directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`). The line is gate-checked before writing (1–200 chars, one line, non-blank, no leading/trailing whitespace) and written with no trailing newline, byte-identical across runs. A post-write check re-reads the file and refuses to finish unless it matches the gate-checked line exactly.

Run it:

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
